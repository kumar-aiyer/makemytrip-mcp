# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

## Expected

- Be civil. Disagree with the code, the design or the reasoning, not with the person.
- Assume the other person is trying to help, especially when their English, their timezone
  or their experience level differs from yours.
- Be specific. "This is wrong" helps nobody; "this returns a per-night rate where the caller
  expects a stay total, see line 40" helps immediately.
- Accept that a maintained project says no to most things. A closed pull request is not an
  insult.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information — including pasting logs that contain someone
  else's searches, addresses or travel dates.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled arguments, demanding unpaid work, or pressuring a
  maintainer for a timeline.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other space
belonging to this project, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (`@kumar-aiyer`) may edit, hide, or delete comments and contributions that
break these rules, and may block accounts that do so repeatedly or seriously.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository, or open an issue if
it is not sensitive. Reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity is not
shared.
